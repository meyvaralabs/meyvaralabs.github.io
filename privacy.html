Privacy Policy

NOCTARA: River of Echoes
Meyvara Labs · Effective date: 10 October 2026
Applies to Android version 1.0.0, package com.meyvara.noctara.

Offline gameplay

NOCTARA is an offline single-player game. It requires no account and includes no advertising, analytics, in-app purchases or automatic crash-reporting service. The game does not transmit personal information, gameplay history or device identifiers to Meyvara Labs or other companies. It does not request Internet, camera, microphone, location, contacts or shared-storage access.

Information stored on your device

The game stores completed stages, stars, equipment, scores, preferences and recent interrupted-passage checkpoints in app-private storage. These support progression, settings and resume. Backup copies may be retained to recover damaged saves. Progress codes contain game progress, not your name or account information; you decide whether to copy or share one.

Android backup

Android may back up or transfer the game's progress preferences according to your device/account settings. This is an operating-system service, not a Meyvara Labs server. Meyvara Labs cannot access those backups. Manage or delete system backups using your device or backup provider's controls.

Vibration and motion

The VIBRATE permission provides optional gameplay feedback. You can disable it in Settings. Optional motion steering reads device orientation locally while enabled. The game does not record or transmit a sensor history. Touch controls remain available.

Retention, deletion and security

Local progress remains until overwritten or deleted. To remove it, use Android Settings → Apps → NOCTARA → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the app. Manage cloud backups separately; Android may restore them on reinstall. Export a progress code first if you want to keep your progress. There is no online account to delete. Local data uses Android's app sandbox, and no game telemetry is sent over a network.

Support communications

If you voluntarily email support, the message and details you include are processed to answer your request and diagnose the issue. Do not include passwords or sensitive information. Support correspondence is retained only as needed to handle the request and applicable obligations; you may request deletion through the same contact. This is separate from the offline game's operation.

Audience and changes

The game contains fictional peril and frightening encounters. It is intended for a teenage and adult audience and does not collect children's personal information through the app. If data practices change, Meyvara Labs will update this policy and the store disclosures.
